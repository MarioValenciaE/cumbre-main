# Positioning

> **Status: provisional.** Language below is a working draft for internal alignment, not approved marketing copy.

## Current positioning

Cumbre is a technology partner for Colombian SMBs that still run important operations with manual, fragmented, or outdated methods. We start by understanding how the business actually works, then recommend the simplest technology that produces measurable value — which may be a spreadsheet fix, an integration, a dashboard, an automation, custom software, or AI, or nothing at all.

## Value proposition (provisional)

- **Diagnosis before solution.** We learn the business first ([discovery methodology](../../PROJECT_CONTEXT.md)).
- **Value over novelty.** We recommend technology only when it moves a business metric.
- **Simple and maintainable.** We weigh build cost and ongoing maintenance cost together.
- **Practical experience.** The founders have built operational tooling (dashboards, ERP integrations, reporting, wholesale systems) in a Colombian retail business.
- **Not locked to one service.** The offering follows the problems we find.

## Who it is for (initial)

Small and medium-sized businesses in Colombia. Specific industries, company sizes, and buyer personas are not yet defined.

## Provisional language (Spanish, to validate)

- "Tecnología práctica para empresas colombianas."
- "Primero entendemos tu negocio, después proponemos soluciones."

## Guardrails

- Do not claim customers, results, ROI, or testimonials Cumbre does not have.
- Do not present past in-house work at Inversiones Diomardi as an independent paying client engagement unless confirmed.
- Do not promise AI/automation as the default answer.
