# Service Hypotheses

> **These are hypotheses, not launched products.** No service catalog or pricing is approved. Categories overlap on purpose; real engagements will mix them.

## Categories

| Category | What it could cover | Typical signals in a business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business intelligence & analytics | Dashboards, KPI monitoring, reporting, scheduled data processing | Decisions made from memory or manual spreadsheets; no single view of sales/inventory |
| Process automation | Replacing repetitive manual tasks, document/data entry, notifications | Staff re-typing data; recurring errors; work stuck on one person |
| AI implementation | Assistants, classification, extraction, forecasting — only where it beats simpler options | Unstructured text/documents; high-volume repetitive judgement calls |
| Custom software | Internal tools, catalogs, wholesale/ordering systems | No off-the-shelf tool fits the workflow |
| Systems integration | Connecting ERP, e-commerce, spreadsheets, messaging, payments | Same data entered in several systems; inconsistent numbers |
| Digital transformation | Roadmaps for moving off paper/manual processes | Management ambition without a clear plan |
| Operational optimization | Process redesign, sometimes with no software at all | Bottlenecks, dependencies on individuals, lost opportunities |

## Evidence from founders' experience (Inversiones Diomardi)

Informs, but does not prove demand for, the first four categories: dashboards, sales/inventory processing, ERP integrations, KPI monitoring, digital wholesale, scheduled reporting, catalog management.

## How to evaluate a hypothesis

1. Has it appeared in more than one discovery conversation?
2. Is the business impact measurable?
3. Can it be delivered simply and maintained affordably?
4. Is any part reusable across clients? (Possible SaaS later.)

## Open questions

- Packaged diagnostic/pilot offering vs. fully custom proposals?
